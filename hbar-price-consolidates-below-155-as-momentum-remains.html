What is Price Consolidation?

Price consolidation refers to a period where the price of an asset, in this case, HBAR, moves within a relatively tight range without showing a clear trend in either direction. This phase is often characterized by low trading volumes and a balance between buyers and sellers. Consolidation is a common occurrence in financial markets and can be seen as a period of rest before the next significant price movement.

  • Definition: A period of price stability within a specific range.
  • Characteristics: Low volatility and trading volumes.
  • Significance: Often precedes a breakout or breakdown in price.
