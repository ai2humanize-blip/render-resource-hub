Introduction to Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR)

Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR) is a decentralized public network that offers a new form of distributed consensus. Unlike traditional blockchain technology, Hedera uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG) to achieve consensus, which allows for high transaction speeds and low fees. The native cryptocurrency of the Hedera network is HBAR, which is used to power decentralized applications, secure the network, and facilitate transactions.
