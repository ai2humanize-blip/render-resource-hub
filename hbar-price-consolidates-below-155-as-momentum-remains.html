What is Price Consolidation?

Price consolidation refers to a period where the price of an asset trades within a tight range, often following a more volatile period. This phase is typically marked by low trading volumes and a balance between buyers and sellers. Consolidation can be seen as a pause in the market, where investors and traders take a breath after a significant price movement, whether upward or downward.

During consolidation, the asset's price tends to bounce between a specific high and low point, creating a trading range. This range can be narrow or wide, depending on the asset's volatility and market conditions. For Hbar, the consolidation is happening below the $0.155 mark, indicating a potential resistance level at this price point.
