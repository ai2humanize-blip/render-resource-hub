Here are some key points to understand about price consolidation:

  • Market Sentiment: It can reflect investor sentiment, which may be uncertain or waiting for a catalyst to drive the price in a particular direction.
  • Volume: During consolidation, trading volume typically decreases, as traders wait for clearer signals before making significant moves.
