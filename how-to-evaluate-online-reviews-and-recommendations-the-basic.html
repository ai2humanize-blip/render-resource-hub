How to Evaluate Online Reviews and Recommendations: the basics

The web rewards readers who slow down, ask questions, and know how to weigh what they discover.

Unfortunately, not all reviews are honest. Here are some common signs of biased or manipulated reviews:

  • Detailed Descriptions: Reviews that provide specific details about the product or service are more likely to be genuine and useful. Look for reviews that mention particular features, pros, and cons.
  • Balanced Feedback: Be wary of reviews that are overwhelmingly positive or negative. A balanced review that acknowledges both strengths and weaknesses is usually more reliable.
  • Consistency: Check if the review aligns with other reviews for the same product or service. If a review stands out as significantly different, it might be an outlier or a fake.

By following these strategies, you can become a more discerning consumer of online reviews and recommendations, helping you make better-informed decisions.

  • Reputation of the Platform: Well-known platforms like Yelp, TripAdvisor, and Amazon have established reputations and tend to have more robust review systems in place. However, even these platforms can have fake reviews, so always be cautious.
  • Expert Reviews: Look for reviews from experts in the field. For example, if you're buying a camera, reading reviews from photography websites or magazines can provide more in-depth and reliable insights.
  • User-Generated Content: While user-generated reviews can be useful, they can also be biased or fake. Look for reviews that provide detailed information and avoid those that seem overly positive or negative without justification.
  • Verified Purchases: Look for reviews marked as "verified purchase" or similar. These reviews are from people who have actually bought the product, making them more trustworthy.
  • Reviewer History: Check the reviewer's history to see if they have a pattern of leaving reviews. A reviewer with a history of detailed and balanced reviews is more likely to be credible.
  • Response from the Company: Some platforms allow companies to respond to reviews. Look for responses that address concerns or provide additional information, as this can indicate a company's commitment to customer service.
