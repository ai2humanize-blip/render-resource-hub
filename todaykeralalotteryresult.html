What is the Kerala State Lottery?

The Kerala State Lottery was established in 1967 and is the first of its kind in India. It was introduced by the Government of Kerala to curb illegal gambling activities and to provide a source of revenue for the state. The lottery is governed by the Kerala Lotteries Department, which operates under the Finance Ministry of the state government.

The lottery is conducted daily, with different schemes and prize structures. The Kerala State Lottery offers various types of lotteries, each with its own set of rules and prize distributions. Some of the popular lottery schemes include:

How to Check the Kerala Lottery Result Today

Checking the Kerala lottery result is a straightforward process. Here are the steps you need to follow to stay updated with the latest results:

1. Official Website: The most reliable source to check the Kerala lottery result is the official website of the Kerala Lotteries Department. The results are usually declared around 3:00 PM every day. Visit the website and navigate to the "Lottery Results" section to find the latest update.

2. Newspapers: The results of the Kerala State Lottery are also published in leading Malayalam newspapers such as Malayala Manorama, Mathrubhumi, and Kerala Kaumudi. You can check the results in the lottery results section of these newspapers.

3. Mobile Apps: There are several mobile applications available on both Android and iOS platforms that provide real-time updates on Kerala lottery results. These apps are user-friendly and offer additional features like notifications and past results.

4. Local Agents: Many local lottery agents also provide the results to their customers. You can visit your nearest lottery shop to inquire about the results.

Types of Kerala State Lotteries

The Kerala State Lottery offers a variety of lotteries, each with its own unique features and prize structures. Here are some of the most popular ones:

  • Win-Win: Conducted on Mondays, this lottery offers a first prize of ₹65 lakhs.
  • Sthree Sakthi: Held on Tuesdays, the first prize for this lottery is ₹50 lakhs.
  • Akshaya: This lottery is conducted on Wednesdays and offers a first prize of ₹70 lakhs.
  • Karunya Plus: Held on Thursdays, the first prize for this lottery is ₹80 lakhs.
  • Nirmal: Conducted on Fridays, this lottery offers a first prize of ₹60 lakhs.
  • Karunya: Held on Saturdays, the first prize for this lottery is ₹1 crore.
  • Pournami: This lottery is conducted on Sundays and offers a first prize of ₹75 lakhs.
