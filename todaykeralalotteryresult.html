What is the Kerala Lottery?

The Kerala State Lottery is a legal form of gambling in the state of Kerala, India. Established in 1967, it is one of the first lotteries to be introduced in India. The lottery is governed by the Directorate of Kerala State Lotteries, which operates under the Department of Finance of the Government of Kerala.

The Kerala Lottery offers a wide range of games, including:

  • Win-Win
  • Sthree Sakthi
  • Akshaya
  • Karunya Plus
  • Nirmal
  • Kerala Karunya
  • Pournami

Each of these games has its own draw date and prize structure, offering participants multiple chances to win.

Understanding the Prize Structure

The Kerala Lottery offers a tiered prize structure, with different prizes for different combinations of numbers. The prize money varies depending on the lottery game. Here is a general overview of the prize structure:

  • 1st Prize: The highest prize, often amounting to several lakhs of rupees, is awarded to the ticket holder with the exact winning number.
  • 2nd Prize: This prize is given to the ticket holders with numbers closely matching the 1st prize number.
  • 3rd Prize: Similar to the 2nd prize, but with a smaller monetary value.
  • Consolation Prizes: These are smaller prizes given to ticket holders with numbers that partially match the winning number.
  • Lower Tier Prizes: Various other prizes are awarded for different number combinations, ensuring that multiple participants can win.

It's important to note that the prize structure can vary between different lottery games, so it's advisable to check the specific rules and regulations for each game.

Claiming Your Prize

If you find that you have won a prize, you will need to follow a few steps to claim it:

  1. Verify Your Ticket: Ensure that your ticket is valid and that the numbers match the winning numbers exactly.
  2. Visit the Lottery Office: Take your winning ticket to the nearest Kerala State Lottery office or an authorized agent.
  3. Fill Out the Claim Form: You will be required to fill out a claim form with your personal details and ticket information.
  4. Provide ID Proof: Submit a copy of your identity proof, such as an Aadhaar card or passport, along with your claim form.
  5. Collect Your Prize: Once your claim is verified, you will receive your prize money.

It's crucial to claim your prize within 30 days of the draw date, as prizes are typically forfeited if not claimed within this period.
