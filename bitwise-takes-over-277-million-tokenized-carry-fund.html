Understanding Tokenization and the Carry Fund

Tokenization is the process of converting rights to an asset into a digital token on a blockchain. This method allows for fractional ownership, increased liquidity, and easier transferability of assets. The $277 million carry fund, now under Bitwise's management, is a testament to the growing acceptance and application of tokenization in the financial world.

A carry fund, in traditional finance, is an investment vehicle that seeks to profit from the difference in yield between long and short positions. In the context of cryptocurrency, a tokenized carry fund operates similarly but leverages blockchain technology to offer enhanced transparency, security, and efficiency.
