Understanding Tokenized Carry Funds

A tokenized carry fund is a type of investment vehicle that leverages blockchain technology to tokenize the performance of an underlying asset or portfolio. In this context, "carry" refers to the return generated from holding an asset, often in the form of interest, dividends, or other income. Tokenization allows for the fractional ownership of these assets, making it easier for investors to diversify their portfolios and gain exposure to various asset classes.

The 277 million tokenized carry fund that Bitwise has acquired is a substantial fund that has been designed to provide investors with a steady income stream through a diversified portfolio of digital assets. The fund's strategy focuses on generating carry by investing in a mix of cryptocurrencies, decentralized finance (DeFi) projects, and other blockchain-based assets.
