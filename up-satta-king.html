History of Satta King

The roots of Satta King can be traced back to the 1960s in India. It began as a form of lottery during the times of the Ankada Jugar, which means "figures gambling." The game evolved over the years and became more organized, with a set of rules and a structured format. Initially, Satta King was played using slips of paper with numbers written on them. These slips were drawn from a large earthenware pot, and the person who had the number on the drawn slip would win the game. Over time, the process became more sophisticated, with the introduction of computerized systems and online platforms to manage the betting and draw processes.
