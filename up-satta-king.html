What is Satta King?

Satta King is a type of lottery game that involves betting on numbers. The game originated in the 1950s when people used to place bets on the opening and closing rates of cotton transmitted from the New York Cotton Exchange to the Bombay Cotton Exchange. Over time, the game evolved, and today, it is played with numbers drawn from a pot or through a random number generator.

The game is typically organized by a "Matka King," who is responsible for collecting the bets and announcing the winning numbers. Participants choose a number between 0 and 99 and place their bets. If their chosen number is drawn, they win a predetermined amount based on the odds set by the Matka King.
