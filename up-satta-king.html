Origins and Evolution of Satta Matka

Satta Matka originated in the 1950s in Mumbai, India. It started as a simple lottery game based on the opening and closing rates of cotton traded on the New York Cotton Exchange. Over time, the game evolved and became more popular, spreading to different parts of the country.

In the early days, the game was played by drawing a card from a deck and players would bet on the number on the card. However, as the game grew in popularity, it transitioned to a more sophisticated system involving the drawing of numbers from a pot, hence the name "Matka."
