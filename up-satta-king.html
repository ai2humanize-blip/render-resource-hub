What is Satta Matka?

Satta Matka began as a form of lottery that involved betting on the opening and closing rates of cotton transmitted from the New York Cotton Exchange. Over time, it transitioned to a more straightforward format involving the random selection of numbers. Participants place bets on numbers, and if their chosen number is drawn, they win a predetermined amount of money.

The term "Satta King" is used to denote the person who has won the most in a particular session or the game itself. It has become synonymous with the idea of winning big in the gambling world.
