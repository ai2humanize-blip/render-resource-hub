History of Satta King

The origins of Satta King can be traced back to the 1950s in India. It started as a form of betting on the opening and closing rates of cotton traded on the New York Cotton Exchange. The game was initially known as "Ankada Jugar" and was played by speculating on the prices of cotton. Over time, the game evolved and shifted to betting on numbers, leading to the creation of various formats and versions.

In the 1960s, the game was popularized by a man named Ratan Khatri, who introduced new rules and made it more organized. The game became known as Satta Matka, with "Satta" meaning betting and "Matka" referring to the pot from which numbers were drawn. The popularity of Satta Matka grew rapidly, especially in Mumbai, where it became a significant part of the local culture.
