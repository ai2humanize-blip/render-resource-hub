What is Satta Matka?

Satta Matka originated in the 1950s and 1960s in India, initially as a form of betting on the opening and closing rates of cotton traded on the New York Cotton Exchange. Over time, it evolved into a lottery system where numbers were drawn from a matka, a large earthenware pot. The game became popular in Mumbai and other parts of India, leading to the development of various formats and variations.

Today, Satta Matka is a popular form of gambling that involves betting on numbers. The game is illegal in India, but it continues to thrive underground due to its widespread appeal and the potential for high returns.
