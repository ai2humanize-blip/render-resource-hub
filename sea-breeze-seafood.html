Sea Breeze Seafood: A Practical Guide for Choosing and Enjoying Seafood

Choosing a seafood market or restaurant comes down to freshness, transparency, and how well the food is prepared. For anyone considering Sea Breeze Seafood, a few practical questions can help you make the most of the visit. Seafood inventories, menus, and hours can change, so checking current information before you go is always sensible.

How to Judge Freshness

Do not rely on appearance alone, but do pay attention to it. At a seafood counter, look for clear, firm flesh, intact edges, a clean and briny smell, and properly chilled displays. Avoid fish with dry patches, discoloration, bruising, or an overly strong odor. Frozen seafood can also be high quality when it is solidly frozen, free from freezer burn, and labeled clearly.

  • Whole fish: Check that the eyes look clear, the gills are fresh, and the skin is moist and bright.
  • Fillets: Look for firm texture and clean-cut edges rather than excessive surface ice or dry areas.
  • Shellfish: Choose live crabs and lobsters that respond to touch. For clams and oysters, make sure shells close when gently tapped and are kept properly chilled.
