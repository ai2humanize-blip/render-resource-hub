One of the most effective ways to verify information is to look for multiple sources that report the same facts. If several reputable sources are saying the same thing, it's more likely to be accurate. that said, be cautious of:

  • Are there statistics or data to support the claims?
  • Are the sources of the data cited and credible?
  • Is there a logical flow to the argument?

Be wary of sources that make sweeping generalizations without supporting evidence. Similarly, be cautious of articles that rely heavily on anonymous sources or unverified information.
