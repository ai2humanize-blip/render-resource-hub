Understanding the BlackRock Bitcoin ETF

A Bitcoin ETF is a financial product that tracks the price of Bitcoin and trades on a traditional stock exchange. It allows investors to gain exposure to Bitcoin without the need to directly purchase, store, and manage the digital currency. BlackRock's entry into the Bitcoin ETF market is significant due to its size, influence, and reputation in the financial industry.

BlackRock filed for a spot Bitcoin ETF in June 2023, which was a landmark moment in the crypto industry. Unlike futures-based Bitcoin ETFs, a spot Bitcoin ETF would directly track the price of Bitcoin, providing investors with a more straightforward way to invest in the cryptocurrency.
