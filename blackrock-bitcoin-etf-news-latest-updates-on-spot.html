What is a Spot Bitcoin ETF?

A spot Bitcoin ETF is a type of exchange-traded fund that would allow investors to buy shares that directly track the current price of Bitcoin, also known as the "spot" price. Unlike futures-based Bitcoin ETFs, which track Bitcoin futures contracts, a spot Bitcoin ETF would provide investors with direct exposure to the cryptocurrency without the need to own or store the actual coins.

The introduction of a spot Bitcoin ETF is seen as a significant step towards mainstream adoption of Bitcoin, as it would offer a more accessible and regulated way for investors to gain exposure to the asset.
