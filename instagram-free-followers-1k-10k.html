Build a Profile That Makes Following Easy

Before promoting posts, make your profile credible and focused. Use a recognizable profile image, a clear name, and a concise bio that explains who you help and what content people can expect. A call to action can tell visitors exactly what to do next, such as saving your newest guide or following for regular updates.

Your content should have a recognizable theme. A focused account is easier for the right people to understand than a general feed assembled around unrelated trends. Keep the most useful posts easy to find by organizing them into Highlights.

Measure Progress by Quality, Not Follower Count

Growing toward 10,000 followers takes time, but quality compounds.
