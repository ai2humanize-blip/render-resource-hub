What to Consider When Evaluating Lumora Lighting

If your interest is a Lumora-branded lighting product, evaluate the specification that matches your space rather than choosing primarily on appearance or wattage. Brightness is usually measured in lumens, which indicate total light output, while wattage describes power consumption. Two products can therefore use similar power but produce noticeably different brightness or beam patterns.

  • Light output and beam angle: Choose output appropriate for the room and select a beam angle that suits the intended location.
  • Color temperature: Warm, neutral, and cool settings create different atmospheres and may affect task visibility.
  • Color rendering: A high color-rendering index is useful where accurate colors matter, such as artwork, photography, retail displays, or food preparation.
  • Electrical compatibility: Confirm input voltage, connector type, and compatibility with existing fixtures, dimmers, smart-home systems, or controllers.
  • Installation rating: For exposed or damp locations, check for an appropriate environmental or wet-location rating.
  • Control options: Decide whether switching, dimming, scheduling, voice control, or app-based operation is necessary.
