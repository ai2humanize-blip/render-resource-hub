What is XRP and Ripple?

XRP is the native digital asset of the Ripple network, a technology designed to enable fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, which are decentralized cryptocurrencies, XRP operates on a more centralized model, with Ripple Labs playing a significant role in its development and distribution.

Ripple's primary goal is to facilitate cross-border payments by providing a more efficient alternative to traditional banking systems. The Ripple network uses XRP as a bridge currency, allowing financial institutions to transfer funds across different currencies quickly and at a lower cost.
