What is XRP?

XRP is a digital asset designed for payments. It is the cryptocurrency used by the Ripple payment network, which facilitates fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin, which was created as an alternative to traditional currencies, XRP was developed to complement existing financial systems by providing a more efficient way to send money globally.

  • Speed: XRP transactions settle in 3-5 seconds, making it one of the fastest cryptocurrencies for payments.
  • Cost: The cost of transacting with XRP is significantly lower than traditional methods, often just a fraction of a cent.
  • Scalability: The Ripple network can handle up to 1,500 transactions per second, far surpassing many other cryptocurrencies.
