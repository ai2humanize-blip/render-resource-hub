What is XRP?

XRP is a digital asset created by Ripple Labs, designed to facilitate fast and low-cost cross-border payments. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, which rely on a proof-of-work consensus mechanism, XRP uses a unique consensus protocol called XRP Ledger Consensus Protocol. This allows for faster transaction times and lower fees, making it an attractive option for financial institutions and payment providers.

While XRP is often referred to as "Ripple," it's important to note that Ripple is the company behind the XRP cryptocurrency. Ripple's technology is used by various financial institutions for remittances and liquidity management.
