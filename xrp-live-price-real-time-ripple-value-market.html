What is XRP?

XRP is a digital currency that was created by Ripple Labs, a technology company focused on providing fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, which are decentralized, XRP operates on the Ripple protocol, a centralized platform designed for financial institutions. The primary purpose of XRP is to facilitate cross-border transactions and act as a bridge currency between different fiat currencies.
