What is XRP and Ripple?

XRP is the native cryptocurrency of the Ripple network, a digital payment protocol that aims to facilitate fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, XRP does not rely on a traditional blockchain but instead uses a distributed ledger technology called the XRP Ledger. Ripple, the company behind XRP, provides solutions for cross-border payments and liquidity management for financial institutions.

Understanding the distinction between XRP and Ripple is important. XRP is the digital asset, while Ripple is the company that developed the XRP Ledger and offers various financial products and services. This distinction is crucial for understanding the dynamics of the XRP market.
