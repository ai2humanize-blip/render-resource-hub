What is XRP and Ripple?

XRP is the native digital asset of the Ripple network, a technology designed to facilitate fast, low-cost international money transfers. Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, XRP does not rely on a traditional proof-of-work or proof-of-stake consensus mechanism. Instead, it uses a unique protocol called the XRP Ledger Consensus Protocol, which allows for faster transaction times and greater scalability.

Ripple, the company behind XRP, provides solutions for cross-border payments and liquidity management for financial institutions. While XRP is often referred to as "Ripple" due to its association with the company, it is important to distinguish between the digital asset and the company itself.
