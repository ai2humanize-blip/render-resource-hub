Current Value of Pi Network

As of now, Pi Network's native cryptocurrency, Pi Coin, is not listed on any major cryptocurrency exchanges. This means that it does not have a publicly traded price. The project is still in its beta phase, and the coins mined by users are not yet transferable or exchangeable for other currencies. The developers have stated that the mainnet launch will be a significant milestone, after which Pi Coins may be listed on exchanges and have a tangible value.

Despite the lack of a formal market price, the value of Pi Coin is often a topic of discussion among its users. The value is currently speculative and based on the project's potential and user trust. Users are encouraged to continue mining and participating in the network with the understanding that the value will be determined by market forces once the coin is tradable.
