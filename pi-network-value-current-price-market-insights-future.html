What is the Pi Network?

The Pi Network was launched in 2019 by a team of Stanford graduates with the vision of creating a cryptocurrency that can be mined using a smartphone, thus making it accessible to a broader audience. Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin that require substantial computational power and energy, Pi aims to lower the barrier to entry by allowing users to mine coins through a simple mobile app.

The project operates on a consensus algorithm called Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP), which is designed to be energy-efficient and secure. The Pi Network is currently in its beta phase, with plans to transition to a fully decentralized mainnet in the future.

Current Price and Value of Pi

As of now, the Pi Network is not listed on any major cryptocurrency exchanges, and there is no official market price for Pi coins. The coins mined by users are stored within the Pi app and cannot be traded or exchanged for other cryptocurrencies or fiat currencies. This lack of liquidity makes it challenging to assign a concrete value to Pi.

However, the value of Pi is often speculated based on its potential adoption, user base growth, and future utility. The developers have emphasized that the value of Pi will be determined by the market once it is listed on exchanges and becomes tradeable. Until then, the focus remains on building a robust community and developing the network's infrastructure.
