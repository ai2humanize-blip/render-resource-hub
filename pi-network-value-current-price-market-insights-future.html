What is Pi Network?

Pi Network was launched by a team of Stanford graduates in 2019 with the vision of creating a cryptocurrency that can be mined using a smartphone. Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, which require substantial computational power and energy, Pi aims to make mining feasible for everyday users. The network operates on a consensus algorithm called Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP), which is designed to be energy-efficient and scalable.

  • Accessibility: Pi Network allows users to mine coins through a simple app, making it accessible to a broader audience.
  • Energy Efficiency: The mining process is designed to consume minimal battery and data, making it environmentally friendly.
  • Community-Driven: The project emphasizes building a strong, engaged community to support its growth and development.
