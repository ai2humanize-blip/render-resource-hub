Installation and Maintenance

Installing Ivanaturf is a straightforward process, but it requires careful planning and execution to ensure optimal results. Here are some general steps involved in the installation:

  • Site Preparation: Clear the area of any debris, rocks, or existing vegetation. Level the ground to create a smooth surface.
  • Base Installation: Lay down a base layer of crushed stone or sand to provide stability and drainage.
  • Turf Installation: Roll out the Ivanaturf and trim it to fit the area. Secure the edges with nails or adhesive.
  • Infill Application: Spread a layer of infill material, such as sand or rubber, to help the turf stand upright and provide additional stability.
  • Final Touches: Brush the turf to ensure the blades are standing upright and the infill is evenly distributed.

Maintenance for Ivanaturf is minimal. Regular brushing to keep the blades upright and occasional rinsing to remove dust and debris are usually sufficient. For pet owners, prompt cleaning of pet waste is recommended to maintain hygiene.
