How to Build a Personal Reading List: the basics

When you spend time online, the difference between a page that helps and one that wastes your time comes down to a few simple signals.

By identifying your preferences, you can create a list that aligns with your tastes and keeps you engaged.

  • Prioritize: Decide which books you want to read first based on your interests and goals. You might choose to read books that are most relevant to your current life situation or those that have been highly recommended.
  • Categorize: Group books by genre, topic, or any other criteria that makes sense to you. This can make it easier to choose your next read based on your mood or current interests.
  • Track your progress: Keep a record of the books you've read and your thoughts on them. This can be done in a journal, on a blog, or using a reading tracking app.

Seek out recommendations from a variety of sources to discover new books and authors. Here are some ideas:

  • Expand your knowledge: Choose books that cover topics you want to learn more about. This could include biographies, historical accounts, or scientific explorations.
  • Improve your skills: Select books that can help you develop specific skills, such as leadership, communication, or time management.
  • Explore new perspectives: Include books that offer different cultural, social, or philosophical viewpoints to broaden your understanding of the world.
  • Enjoyment and relaxation: Don't forget to include books that you simply find entertaining or relaxing. Reading should be a pleasurable experience, after all.

Creating a personal reading list is a rewarding endeavor that can enrich your life, broaden your horizons, and provide a sense of accomplishment. Whether you're a casual reader or a voracious bookworm, having a structured reading list can help you stay organized and motivated. While it's key to have a plan, don't be afraid to be flexible. Life is unpredictable, and your reading preferences may change over time. Allow yourself the freedom to deviate from your list and explore new books that catch your interest. After all, the joy of reading lies in the journey, not just the destination.

By incorporating recommendations into your list, you can ensure a diverse and exciting reading experience.

  • Friends and family: Ask people you trust for their favorite book recommendations. They might introduce you to genres or authors you haven't considered before.
  • Book clubs: Joining a book club can expose you to a wide range of books and provide insightful discussions that enhance your reading experience.
  • Online platforms: Websites like Goodreads, Amazon, and LibraryThing offer personalized recommendations based on your reading history and preferences.
  • Literary awards and bestsellers: Check out lists of award-winning books and bestsellers to discover highly regarded and popular titles.
